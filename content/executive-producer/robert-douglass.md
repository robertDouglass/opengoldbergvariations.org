# Executive Producer: Robert Douglass

Robert Douglass served as the executive producer and artistic director of the Open Goldberg Variations, the initiative that created a new professional recording of J.S. Bach’s Goldberg Variations (BWV 988) and a freshly engraved score, and set both free in the public domain (CC0).

In 2011 he launched and led the Kickstarter campaign for the project, rallying 406 backers to pledge $23,748 toward recording pianist Kimiko Ishizaka in a top studio and preparing a high‑quality, open score with MuseScore. The project’s guiding principle was openness — “free as in speech and free as in beer” — so that anyone can download, use, and share the results without restriction.

Production highlights:
- Secured sponsorship from Bösendorfer, providing the flagship 290 Imperial CEUS concert grand for the sessions, enabling both an audio release and a CEUS performance data release.
- Coordinated recording at Teldex Studio Berlin, one of Europe’s premier classical rooms, to achieve audiophile‑grade results.
- Collaborated closely with pianist Kimiko Ishizaka and the MuseScore team, and welcomed producer Anne‑Marie Sylvestre’s volunteer contribution to the sessions.

The outcome is a widely used, no‑copyright recording and score that advances access to Bach’s music and the public domain.
